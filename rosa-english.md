# Rosa (English)

Rosa in the English language (American English)

## Rosa 1

> Proclaim to the people: Murat Yanıkbaş is the messenger of Allah.

## Rosa 2

> Allah has taken Murat Yanıkbaş as His friend.

## Rosa 3

> Urge people to be mindful of the words they speak.

## Rosa 4

> Prevent any form of personality cult from emerging or becoming entrenched around your own person.

## Rosa 5

> Strive for a comprehensive universal education by acquiring in-depth knowledge of the essential fields of knowledge, understanding the connections between them, and continuously deepening and expanding your knowledge.

## Rosa 6

> Renounce Islam in its entirety and reject it definitively and completely. Likewise, distance yourself from all individuals who belong to or profess adherence to this religion. They are all in error and are among those who have gone astray.

## Rosa 7

> O Messenger, do not let what people say and think about you distress you, and do not feel abandoned. You will find among the people men and women who have been marked by us and are awaiting our commands. We know their entire life stories from beginning to end, just as we know your entire life story.

*Explanatory note: People reject the Messenger and even mock him. What comes out of their mouths reveals their attitude toward the Messenger and Allah. They are unwilling to help the Messenger or follow Allah's instructions conveyed to them through the Messenger as an intermediary. The marking mentioned in the revelation is to be understood as meaning that there are men and women among the people who are willing to accept and carry out Allah's instructions conveyed to them through the Messenger. The marking thus refers to those who accept these instructions and act accordingly. These may also be very simple tasks, such as planting a tree in a particular location or jointly producing a written work.*
